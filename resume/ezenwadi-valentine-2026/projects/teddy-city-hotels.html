07 / Frontend case note · Hotel experience

Teddy City Hotels.

A guest-facing hotel presence connected to the practical work of rooms, bookings, amenities, food service, and staff operations.

Role
Lead frontend developer
Product surface
Public website and hotel operations
Sector
Hotel experience
Frontend focusApplication design and implementation

Guest and staff workflows.

Hospitality interfaces need to be warm and useful. Guests should understand what is available and how to book; staff need clear operational views behind that experience. My frontend lens connected those two sides without making the public site feel like an admin tool.

01

Public experience

Made rooms, amenities, and booking entry points easy to find on the public site.

02

Staff operations

Organized room, booking, kitchen, swimming, snooker, and revenue views around daily use.

03

Service status

Presented booking and service states so staff could identify the next action.

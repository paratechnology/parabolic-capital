CityVibe.

One product family spanning city discovery, venue activity, orders, reservations, and the tools businesses use to operate.

Role
Lead frontend developer
Product surface
Consumer, business, and admin frontends
Sector
Local commerce
Frontend focusApplication design and implementation

Interfaces for distinct audiences.

CityVibe serves different people with different goals. Consumers want to discover and act quickly; venues need to manage activity; admins need oversight. My frontend focus was giving each audience a coherent surface while keeping shared objects and states recognizable.

01

Audience-specific flows

Separated discovery, business operations, and admin control with appropriate navigation and hierarchy.

02

Connected actions

Connected venue detail to orders, reservations, events, and account activity.

03

Live status

Made order and reservation changes visible in the interfaces used during operations.
